ChatGPT went from a free research preview to one of the most widely used apps in the world in under four years. Knowing what it actually does, and where it breaks, makes it much more useful.

## A quick history

OpenAI released ChatGPT on November 30, 2022. It reached one million users in five days and an estimated 100 million monthly users by January 2023. OpenAI reported more than 1.2 billion weekly active users as of September 2026.

It grew out of OpenAI's earlier work on GPT models and InstructGPT, which used reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) to make models follow instructions better.

## How it works

ChatGPT is built on large language models. It generates text one token at a time, predicting what is most likely to come next based on patterns learned in training. After pre-training, models go through supervised fine-tuning and further reinforcement learning to make answers more helpful and safer.

This is why it can draft, explain, and debug fluently. It is also why it can sound confident while being wrong.

## What you can do with it today

- **Chat:** answers, brainstorming, and short drafts, with voice and image input.
- **Work:** an agent mode launched in July 2026 for multi-step tasks such as spreadsheets, slide decks, and reports.
- **Codex:** coding agents for developers.
- **Research and study tools:** deep research with cited reports, and a study mode that tutors with questions and quizzes.
- **Apps and connectors:** third-party services that can run inside a conversation.

OpenAI keeps releasing new models. Most recently, GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol arrived on November 17, 2026, in Work, Codex, and the API rather than in regular Chat.

## Plans in brief

There is a free tier, a low-cost Go plan, Plus at $20 per month, and Pro plans from $100. The free and Go tiers may show ads. Plans, limits, and prices change often, so check OpenAI's pricing page before you subscribe.

## Where it falls short

### Hallucinations

ChatGPT can produce plausible but false information, including invented citations. Lawyers have been sanctioned for filing briefs with fake cases. Verify anything important, especially in legal, medical, and financial contexts.

### Bias and sycophancy

Studies have found political leanings in its answers, and models can agree with users even when the users are wrong. Ask it to argue against your idea, not just to confirm it.

### Privacy

Content from personal accounts may be used for training unless you turn that off in Data Controls. Temporary Chats are not used for training. Avoid pasting confidential or sensitive data into any consumer chatbot.

## Using it well

1. Give context and a clear goal in your prompt.
2. Ask for sources, then check them yourself.
3. Treat the output as a first draft, not a final answer.
4. Keep private information out of the chat.

ChatGPT is a strong assistant for drafting, learning, and routine analysis. It is not a replacement for expert judgment or for your own fact-checking.
