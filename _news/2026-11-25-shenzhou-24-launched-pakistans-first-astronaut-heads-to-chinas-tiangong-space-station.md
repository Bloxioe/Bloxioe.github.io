Pakistan has taken a historic step into human spaceflight. On the morning of 25 November 2026, China launched its Shenzhou-24 spacecraft, and for the first time a Pakistani astronaut is on his way to a space station.

## What happened?

The spacecraft lifted off from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwestern China at 8:18 am local time. Three people are on board: two Chinese astronauts and one Pakistani astronaut.

The mission is also a first for China. Muhammad Zeeshan Ali is set to become the first foreign astronaut to board China's Tiangong space station.

## Who is on the crew?

- Tang Hongbo (51): Chinese astronaut
- Wu Yi (39): Chinese astronaut
- Muhammad Zeeshan Ali (40): Pakistani astronaut, flying as a payload specialist

A payload specialist is not a regular station crew member. This person travels mainly to carry out specific experiments and tasks during a short stay in orbit.

## How long will the Pakistani astronaut stay in space?

According to the reports, Zeeshan Ali will spend roughly one week on the station. After his stay, he is expected to return to Earth aboard the Shenzhou-23 spacecraft, not the one he launched on.

## Why does this mission matter?

For Pakistan, this is the first time one of its own citizens is flying to space, which makes it a major moment for the country's space programme and for SUPARCO, the national space agency.

For China, it opens the doors of Tiangong to an international partner for the first time. It shows that China wants its space station to be a place for international cooperation, not only for its own astronauts.

It also highlights how close the China-Pakistan partnership has become, now reaching beyond trade and defence into space exploration.

## What happens next?

Now the crew will travel to Tiangong and dock with the station. After that, the focus shifts to the astronaut's work in orbit and his safe return home. We will keep following the mission as new updates arrive.

For many young people in Pakistan, this launch may be the moment that makes a career in space science feel possible. That could be the biggest result of this mission in the long run.
