Please contact the operator of an external website about material hosted there. We can review material published on this site, but cannot remove content from a third-party service.

Report material on this site

Send a clear notice through the contact page or email. Include enough information for us to identify the work and the material you are reporting:

  • Your name and a reliable email address, plus the name of the rights holder if you are acting for someone else
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you believe is infringed
  • The exact URL on this site and a description of the specific material in question
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the rights holder, its agent, or the law
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, where applicable, that you are authorised to act for the rights holder
  • Your physical or electronic signature

We will review complete notices and, where appropriate, remove or restrict access to the material while we assess the request. We may contact you for clarification. Submitting a notice does not guarantee removal, and knowingly false claims may have legal consequences.

Responses and counter-notices

If material is removed or restricted, the person who submitted it may contact us with an explanation and supporting information if they believe this was a mistake. We will review the response and determine whether restoration is appropriate. We may share the notice or response with the affected parties where needed to handle the claim.

Scope and legal information

This page describes how to raise a copyright concern about content on this blog. It is not legal advice and does not create a guarantee about the outcome or timing of a review. Formal legal requirements can depend on the applicable law and location.
