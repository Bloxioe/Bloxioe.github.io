DMCA Notice

We hereby issue this notice to inform you that these extensions just function like an ordinary browser (like your browser) that fetch video files from internet, and do not violate the provisions of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). The Content these extensions may access is not hosted by us or the Pluginstream application but the websites they are browsing in their autonomous mode. It is sole responsibility of the user and his/her countries' or states' law. If you think they are violating any intellectual property then please contact the actual file hosts, we are also ready to remove these extensions at anytime you ask for it.

What PluginStream Is

PluginStream is an open-source client, not a content library. It is closer to a browser than to a streaming service.

  • We do not host, upload, index, store or mirror any video, subtitle or media file
  • Extensions are written and shared by the open-source community in public repositories — anyone can write, inspect or remove one
  • The content you watch lives on third-party websites, reached directly by your device — it never passes through a server we run
  • We do not control those websites, their content, their availability or their legality in your country
  • Which extension you install, and what you choose to open, is solely your decision and your responsibility under the laws of your country or state

Filing a Takedown Request

If you believe an extension listed on this site infringes your rights, we are ready to act. Because no content is hosted by us, the fastest and most effective route is to contact the actual file host serving that content. At the same time, we will remove an extension from the list we publish as soon as you ask.

Please include the following in your request:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work you believe is being infringed
  • The exact extension name, ID or the URL on this site where it is listed
  • Your name, email and contact details
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorised, and that the information in your notice is accurate
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you are the rights holder or authorised to act on their behalf
  • Your physical or electronic signature

Rights holders are welcome to reach us through the contact page or by opening an issue on GitHub. We will act on a valid request — including removing an extension from the list we publish — without delay.

Counter-Notice

If an extension you published was removed and you believe the removal was mistaken, you may send us a counter-notice with the same contact details above. We will review it and may restore the listing where appropriate.
