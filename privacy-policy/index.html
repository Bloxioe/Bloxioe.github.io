Privacy

Privacy Policy

Komi is built privacy-first. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking and no data collection — what happens on your device stays on your device. This page explains that in plain language, and includes the DMCA notice for PluginStream.

Last Updated: November 19, 2026
Version: 3.0
Project: Komi Store — maintained by Abdul Mueed
Contact: Contact page or hello@am-abdulmueed.me

About This Project

Komi is an independent, open-source project. This website (am-abdulmueed.github.io) is a static site that showcases PluginStream — an Android app for movies, TV series and anime — together with its documentation, release notes and community extension list.

PluginStream itself is a simple client. It does not host, store, mirror or re-upload any video, subtitle or media file. Everything it can play comes from third-party websites, reached through extensions written by the open-source community. How you use the app is entirely your choice, and your responsibility under the laws of your own country or state.

TL;DR — The Short Version

  • ✅ No account, no sign-up, no login
  • ✅ We collect no personal data — nothing is copied, stored or sold
  • ✅ No cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts on this website
  • ✅ No ads and no ad networks
  • ✅ PluginStream hosts no content — extensions come from the open-source community
  • ✅ 100% open source and auditable

If a feature ever needs your data, it will ask first — and it will be off unless you turn it on.

What We Don't Collect

❌ Names, emails or any personal information
❌ Accounts, passwords or login credentials
❌ Location data
❌ Device identifiers or advertising IDs
❌ Search, watch or usage history
❌ Data from other apps on your device

This Website

The am-abdulmueed.github.io website is a static site. It is served straight from files — there is no database, no user session, and no server-side profile of you.

  • No cookies are set by this website and no consent banner is needed
  • No third-party analytics, heatmaps, pixels or advertising scripts are loaded
  • Nothing you read, click or copy on this site is recorded by us
  • Pages that show live project data read it directly from the public GitHub API, from your browser to GitHub
  • Like any website host, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical request logs for security and abuse prevention, governed by GitHub's site policies. We have no access to a personal profile built from them

Newsletter (Optional, Website Only)

The blog offers an optional newsletter signup. It is a website-only feature — the PluginStream app never asks for your email address and never subscribes you to anything.

  • What we collect: the email address you type, and the date you subscribed. Nothing else — no tracking pixels, no profiling
  • Provider: Buttondown, an indie, privacy-focused newsletter platform, subject to Buttondown's Privacy Policy
  • How we use it: only to send the newsletter — release notes and project updates. We never sell, rent or share it
  • Unsubscribe: every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, or write to hello@am-abdulmueed.me and we will remove you manually

PluginStream App

PluginStream is a free, open-source Android app. It requires no account, no sign-up and no personal details of any kind.

  • Nothing leaves your device: preferences, favourites, playback settings and the list of installed extensions are stored locally on your phone
  • No telemetry: no usage events, no crash profiles tied to you, no device identifiers
  • Direct connections: when you open a title, your device talks directly to the third-party website serving that content — the traffic never passes through a server we run
  • Uninstall or clear app data and everything PluginStream stored is gone permanently

Extensions and Third-Party Content

PluginStream is not a content library. It is a client, more like a browser than a streaming service.

  • Extensions are created and shared by the open-source community in public repositories. Anyone can write one, inspect one, or remove one
  • We do not host, upload, index, store or mirror any content. The files you watch live on third-party websites, not with us and not inside the PluginStream application
  • We do not control those websites, their content, their availability or their legality in your country
  • Which extension you install, and what you choose to open, is solely your decision and your responsibility under the laws of your country or state
  • Because no content passes through us, any intellectual property or copyright complaint should be directed to the actual file host serving that content

DMCA Notice

We hereby issue this notice to inform you that these extensions just function like an ordinary browser (like your browser) that fetch video files from internet, and do not violate the provisions of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). The Content these extensions may access is not hosted by us or the Pluginstream application but the websites they are browsing in their autonomous mode. It is sole responsibility of the user and his/her countries' or states' law. If you think they are violating any intellectual property then please contact the actual file hosts, we are also ready to remove these extensions at anytime you ask for it.

Rights holders are welcome to reach us through the contact page or by opening an issue on GitHub, and we will act on a valid request — including removing an extension from the list we publish — without delay.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any data, because there is no user data for us to share.

The only third parties involved are the ones you already chose to talk to:

  • GitHub — when this website or the app requests public project data (GitHub's Privacy Policy)
  • Buttondown — only if you personally sign up for the newsletter
  • The third-party websites your extensions connect to, which apply their own policies
  • No advertising networks, no data brokers, no analytics SDKs

Your Rights and Control

You keep full control, because there is nothing stored about you on our side:

  • No account to delete — PluginStream works without signing in
  • Clear app data in Android Settings to erase every locally stored preference and extension
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything permanently
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time with one click
  • Ask us anything about privacy through the contact page — since we hold no personal data, there is nothing to export or erase, but we will always confirm that in writing

Children's Privacy

PluginStream is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone — including children — because the project collects no personal information at all. Parents should know that third-party websites and extensions reachable from the app are outside our control, and the app is intended for adults.
