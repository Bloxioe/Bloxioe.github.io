Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be processed when you read the blog, contact us, subscribe to the newsletter, or encounter advertising on the site.

Last updated: November 20, 2026
Site: Bloxioe's Blog
Contact: Contact page or am.abdulmueed1@gmail.com

Who runs this blog and how we publish

This is Bloxioe's personal technology blog. Posts are prepared and published by the author and may cover current technology and software news, tutorials, open-source work, opinions, and project notes. News-related posts are commentary or summaries, not a substitute for the original reporting; we aim to link to relevant sources and correct material when we learn it is inaccurate.

The RSS feed and the LLM-readable index are generated from the public site content when the site is built. They contain published pages and posts, not visitor profiles or private messages. Reading the blog does not require an account.

Information we may process

  • When you browse: the site host and services used by the page may process technical request information such as IP address, requested page, browser, device, and time for delivery, security, and abuse prevention.
  • When you choose a theme: your display preference is saved in this browser's local storage so it can be remembered. This preference is not sent to us.
  • When you use the contact form: the name, email address, and message you choose to provide are sent to Web3Forms for delivery to the site owner. You can email us directly instead. Please do not include sensitive information.
  • When you subscribe: Buttondown processes the email address you submit and subscription information so it can send the newsletter and manage unsubscribes.
  • When ads are displayed: Adsterra and its advertising partners may process technical and advertising information as described below.

Advertising and Adsterra

This site displays ads served by Adsterra. Advertising scripts and links may connect your browser directly to Adsterra or its partners. Those third parties may use cookies or similar technologies and process information such as your IP address, device or browser details, pages or ads viewed, and ad interactions to deliver, measure, secure, or personalise advertising, depending on their settings and applicable law.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not control the data collected directly by Adsterra or its partners, how long they keep it, or their use of it. Review the Adsterra Privacy Policy for details about their practices and available choices. You can also use your browser's privacy controls or an ad blocker; blocking ads may not prevent every third-party request.

Contact messages

If you contact us, we use the information in your message to read and respond to your request, such as feedback, corrections, story ideas, or business enquiries. The form is provided by Web3Forms, which processes the submission to deliver it. We keep correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to handle the conversation and maintain relevant records, then delete it when no longer needed, subject to routine email backups or legal obligations.

Newsletter

The newsletter is optional. If you subscribe, Buttondown receives your email address and subscription details to send emails and process unsubscribe requests. Buttondown may process technical delivery and engagement data under its own policies. You can unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter or contact us for help. See Buttondown's Privacy Policy.

Hosting, fonts, and other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like other web hosts, GitHub may process technical request data for hosting, security, and abuse prevention under GitHub's Privacy Statement. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts; your browser may connect to Google to retrieve them. Some pages, including the CMS, load scripts from third-party CDNs. Those providers receive the requests needed to deliver their resources and apply their own privacy policies.

We do not run a user account system, sell personal information, or use our own analytics profile to track readers. Third-party services described on this page operate independently and may use their own cookies, logs, or similar technologies.

Your choices and requests

  • Do not use the contact form or newsletter if you do not want to provide an email address; reading public pages does not require one.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using its unsubscribe link.
  • Use browser settings to manage cookies and other storage, and use an ad blocker if you prefer not to load advertising.
  • Contact us to ask about a message you sent or to make a privacy request. We will review it and respond as required by applicable law.

Children

This blog is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the site or its services change. The updated date at the top will change when we publish a revision.
